# WoW Sync v0.4.0 (pre-release)

An **unsigned Windows x64 preview**. Update every computer in the group.
Nothing about add-on settings is turned on by installing this version: you
choose, on the Settings sync page or from the offer below.

## New: sharing add-on settings between your computers

WoW Sync can now keep your add-ons' saved settings (the `SavedVariables`
files in each account and character folder) the same on your computers, the
way it already keeps add-on files the same.

### What is shared, and what is not

- **Shared:** each add-on's saved settings, for every add-on on the computer,
  including add-ons you install later. Account-wide settings stay inside
  their account, and character settings stay inside that character on that
  realm. Settings are matched by the exact account, realm and character
  folder names; a name is never guessed or translated.
- **Not shared:** WoW's own settings (`Config.wtf`), key bindings, macros,
  layout, chat settings and caches. Settings are never merged: when two
  computers changed the same add-on's settings on purpose, you are asked
  which to keep.
- WoW Sync never creates an account, realm or character folder. A character
  that exists only on one computer is shown as waiting (see below).
- Settings are copied only while WoW is closed, and only after WoW has
  finished writing them.

### How to turn it on, step by step

1. Update every computer to v0.4.0 and let add-ons finish syncing. When
   everything is up to date and no decision is waiting, the headline shows
   **"Share add-on settings between your computers?"** with **Set up…** and
   **Not now**. The same offer is always available on the **Settings sync**
   page.
2. On the computer you want to start from, choose **Set up…**. Read the
   explanation, then:
   - Leave the accounts you want to share ticked. Every account found on the
     computer is ticked at first; untick any you do not want to share.
   - Pick the **settings master**. This computer is pre-selected. Pick the
     computer whose add-on settings you trust most, normally the one you play
     on most: its settings become the starting point for your other
     computers, whose own settings are kept as recovery copies before
     anything is replaced.
   - Choose **Share settings**. **Cancel** changes nothing.
   - If some add-ons' settings here differ from the group's or have no shared
     copy, the sheet lists them with **Share these now**. Becoming the master
     does not share them by itself. The box is off, except the very first time
     when the group has no shared settings yet (then it is on). **Make this
     computer the master…** on the Settings sync page asks the same.
3. On each of your other computers, the offer reads **"<computer> is now the
   settings master. Use its add-on settings here?"** with that computer's own
   account list. Choose **Use settings** to join; choose **Not now** to wait.
   Until you confirm, that computer neither publishes nor applies any
   settings.

**Not now** is remembered while the app is open and hides the offer from the
headline. The offer stays on the Settings sync page until settings are turned
on.

### What you see

- The **Add-ons** list has a **Settings** column in plain words, for example
  "Settings up to date (from RYZENFISH)", "Changed here, not shared" or
  "Settings not shared". An add-on's page has a **Settings** panel with one
  line per account or character.
- **Use shared settings** is offered for every add-on that shows "Changed here,
  not shared"; your current settings are kept as a recovery copy.
- A settings file that looks damaged shows "This add-on's settings file looks
  damaged; it was not shared".
- **Waiting for <character> to log in on this computer** means the
  settings are shared but this computer has no folder for that character yet.
  Log in with that character once; the settings are then applied when WoW
  closes.
- **Activity** and each add-on's **Recent changes** list settings changes with
  the same detail as add-on changes: where they came from, the reason and the
  size, and whose settings they are.
- The headline and the tray show progress through the settings step.
- When two copies of an add-on's settings compete, each choice says how the
  copy came about (restored, chosen, pushed or edited in game), where it came
  from (settings master, this computer or another computer), its date and
  time to the second and its size. Choices that would still read alike are
  marked "(older)" and "(newer)". While a choice is waiting, the headline and
  **Needs attention** say so instead of "up to date".
- After you choose a copy or restore one, the headline says "Syncing" until
  this computer has applied it.
- If you change an add-on's settings in game on the settings master after
  choosing or restoring a copy for it, your edit is kept and offered as a
  choice; it is never silently replaced.
- The recovery list shows each saved copy's date, time, size and what replaced
  it. A restore is shown as a restore on both computers.
- An add-on whose saved settings are shared with another add-on reads "Shared
  with <add-on>, not synced".

### Pause, restore and undo

- **Pause all settings** on the Settings sync page stops sharing and applying
  for every add-on on this computer, including add-ons found later, until you
  choose **Resume all settings**. It is remembered between runs.
- **Pause settings** on the Settings sync page stops sharing and applying for
  one add-on until you resume. Resuming shows you any differences first and
  never shares your copy without you choosing **Share my settings**.
- An add-on's page lists the three saved states of its settings with
  **Restore**. Restoring keeps the current copy as a recovery copy, so it can
  be undone.
- **Don't share this add-on** and **Use <computer> for this add-on** are
  per-add-on exceptions to the master. **Change master…** moves the master;
  nothing is chosen for you when the master computer is retired.
- Removing an add-on removes its settings too; both can be restored from the
  Removed list.

## Known limits

- **Not now** is not saved between runs yet; the offer reappears on the
  headline after a restart until settings are turned on.
- Files over the 32 MiB per-file limit are not shared.
- While WoW is running, an add-on whose settings are waiting to be applied
  says "waiting for the game" on its own row, but the main headline can still
  say up to date.
- An add-on's saved settings that are declared by more than one add-on are
  not shared automatically; they are shown with an explanation.
- Settings sharing has been exercised on synthetic data with two computers,
  not on a live group. Turn it on first on a computer whose settings you can
  afford to restore from a recovery copy.
